# Spondee Frontend Design

Status: pre-deployment refinement

## Surface Modes

Marketplace and category pages persuade a cold judge to start the core path. Agent, Promise, Activation, Receipt, Replay and Evidence pages operate as proof surfaces.

## Visual Thesis

Premium DeFi marketplace meets evidence terminal: near-black canvas, quiet layered surfaces, warm off-white text, restrained gold action color, compact proof blocks and direct labels. The UI should feel like a serious user is granting financial authority only after seeing the agent's precommitted promise.

## Copy Tone

Use concrete product language: promise, authority, activation, receipt, replay, observed evidence, baseline. Avoid inflated claims, generic AI phrasing and unsupported performance language. Negative and neutral outcomes are evidence, not failures. SIMULATION and OBSERVED must remain visually and verbally distinct.

## Components

Cards are for meaningful objects: category, agent, Promise Card, receipt panel, evidence run. Proof metadata belongs near the result it validates. Primary actions use verbs and should sit close to the decision they complete.

## Responsive Rules

Mobile stacks proof panels into one column, preserves the main call to action, and avoids horizontal scroll for IDs, JSON, transactions and long backend labels. Tables and definition grids collapse before they become cramped.

## Accessibility

Use semantic headings, links, buttons, form labels and definition lists. Focus states remain visible. Status is expressed with text plus icon/color, never color alone. Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Anti-Patterns

No fabricated evidence counts, hard-coded observed results, fake live indicators, guaranteed profit/safety claims, neon casino styling, generic AI SaaS copy, badge walls or decorative gradients that obscure product truth.
